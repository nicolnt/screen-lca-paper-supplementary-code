# Hotspot products and their bio-based pathways

A graph database representing bio-based alternative pathways to high-greenhouse-gas-footprint petrochemicals in the European Union.

Share link: https://kumu.io/nicolas-lienart/screenlca-paper-biobased-pathways

Author: [Nicolas LIENART](https://orcid.org/0009-0001-3259-2819)

## Legend

Diamonds represent processes interconnecting products, represented by circles.

A complete legend is located in the bottom left corner.

## Metadata

Nodes contain some metadata listed below.

For more details, please refer to the related scientific publication, including its supplementary materials and code.

### Products:

- `Available quantity in the EU27 in 2022 (Mt)`: This accounts both production + imports, expressed in million tonnes of product per year
- `HS 2022 codes`: 6-digit Harmonized System (HS) 2022 codes
- `Hotspot rank`: Priority rank of the product, based on its total footprint, ranking is performed by descending order of total footprint
- `PRODCOM classification`: Associated PRODCOM product, there could be multiple matching products when the hotspot product has been aggregated.
- `Total GHG footprint of the available quantity in the EU27 in 2022 (Mt CO2-eq)`: Indicates the greenhouse gas (GHG) footprint of the product, related to the available quantity. It uses the GWP100 indicator based on characterization factors of the IPCC 2021 report.

### Processes:

- `Maturity score`: Possible options are either "Low", "Medium", "High" or "Fully mature". See article supplementary materials's PDF Table S6 for more details.
- `Maturity score references`: Any bibliographic reference whiched helped to determine the process' maturity
- `Process references`: Any bibliographic reference giving some details about the process
